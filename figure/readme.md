# Figure provenance

`gridworld.png`, `gridworld.svg`, `values.png` and `values.svg` are generated by
`python -m examples.plot_gridworld --output figure` with seed 0 and 1,500 Q-learning episodes.
The exact policy/value/trajectory data are saved alongside them in `gridworld_data.json`.

`benchmark.png` and `benchmark.svg` are produced by `minrl plot` from the full CartPole
benchmark. See `docs/benchmark.md` for configuration, seed-level results and timing.

The older `policy_ppo.png`, `train_results_ppo.png` and `trajectory_ppo.png` are historical
0.1 illustrations. They are retained for provenance, are not generated by this revision,
and are not used as evidence of current performance.
